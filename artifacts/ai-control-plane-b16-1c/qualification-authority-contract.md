3. `schematicAuthorityFor(scope)` — the LAST committed pcb-sync audit's plan snapshot (`schematicRevision`, `schematicDigest`, `planId`, `snapshotDigest`); missing → 422 `MFG_SCHEMATIC_AUTHORITY_MISSING`. [B16-A corrected: F4 — until `3231f3f` parity was certified against `boardToSourceNetlist(board)`, the board against itself, and the board-derived netlist was sealed; `b16-a-snapshot-netlist-parity.test.ts` reproduces a lost net, a gained footprint and a moved endpoint each passing the old check and failing the new; the tripwire forbids `boardToSourceNetlist` in the route]

## The run

- Durable single-flight LEASE per (tenant, project, branch, board): `DurableQualificationLease` (`app/lib/pcb/fab/qualification-lease.ts`, aggregate `mfg.qualification-lease.v1`) — acquisition is a compare-and-swap append on the lease's current revision (two racing acquirers cannot both win; the loser sees `DW_OPTIMISTIC_REVISION_CONFLICT` → `raced`), a held lease refuses new acquirers until released or until its TTL lapses (`QUALIFICATION_LEASE_TTL_MS = 330_000`, longer than the route's own 300 s maximum), release is token-bound (a lapsed holder cannot release its successor's lease), and the holder label is the request's correlation id. A concurrent call is refused 409 `MFG_QUALIFICATION_IN_PROGRESS` (recovery H.11) on EVERY instance of a multi-instance deployment. [B16-A corrected: F7 — until `3231f3f` the guard was a process-local `Map` (`inFlight`, route line 120 at `c82d1cb`), so two instances both ran the router; `b16-a-qualification-commit.test.ts` "F7" cases; the tripwire forbids `new Map<` in the route]
- Declaration: `footprintQualifications` are exactly the server-proven classes; risk classes are the profile's declared envelope; `revision = componentAuthority.digest`.
- Capability pre-screen (`evaluateAutonomousCapability`, `sirkit.autonomous-pcb-capability.v1`) → 422 `MFG_AUTONOMOUS_INELIGIBLE` before any router creation (parity suite).
- `qualifyAutonomousLayout` with the production `AutonomousRouteExecutor` (bundled FreeRouting 2.2.4 on the bundled JRE) over fitted outline candidates smallest-first; the selected verified candidate stays IN MEMORY while RC01 parity (against the applied snapshot), RC02 evidence and the authority seal are computed; then the routed board AND the sealed authority are written in ONE durable commit — `commitQualification(workflow, { boardRecord, expectedBoardAggregateRevision: head.aggregateRevision, authority, expectedAuthorityRevision, idempotencyKey: "qualify:<board>:<from>-><to>:<key>" })` (`app/lib/pcb/fab/qualification-commit.ts`; two operations of one commit, transactional in the PostgreSQL repository, all-or-nothing in memory; both heads are re-read and verified after the commit → `MFG_QUALIFICATION_COMMIT_UNVERIFIED` otherwise) — compare-and-swap on the CURRENT board aggregate revision AND the current authority revision; a conflict is 409 `MFG_BOARD_REVISION_CONFLICT`, never an overwrite; the lease is released in `finally`. [B16-A corrected: F2 — until `3231f3f` the routed board was published durably inside the layout's `commitSelected` callback (route line 214 at `c82d1cb`) BEFORE RC01/RC02 and the authority save, so a later gate failure returned "Nothing was changed" over an already-replaced board; `b16-a-qualification-commit.test.ts` reproduces the fault and pins all-or-nothing on both aggregates; tripwires forbid `publishBoard(` and `commitSelected:` in the route and pin `expectedBoardAggregateRevision` (`fba0e1e`)]
- Deterministic CI: the router is REPLAYED from a recording keyed by the exact placed-board revision (`__tests__/fixtures/route-fixture.ts`; fixtures `blinker-route-fixture.json` (2 executions) and `blinker-route-fixture-recovery.json` (4 executions), recorded locally with `SIRKIT_B16_1C_RECORD_ROUTES=1` — re-recorded in `8746b46` after the F8 locale fix changed every placed-board revision); replay refuses unknown boards (`ROUTE_FIXTURE_MISS`) and every replayed candidate is re-verified by the unchanged verification code (RC01 parity, `verifyAutorouteCandidate`, capability re-evaluation, DRC). The replay seam substitutes the WHOLE production executor, so `boardToDsn`/`validateDsn`/`writeDsn`/`runFreerouting`/`parseSes`/`buildAutorouteCandidate` are exercised only by a live run — opt-in `SIRKIT_B16_1C_LIVE_ROUTER=1` (committed in `850ee3e`, F21; every replay also asserts the pinned identities in `fixtures/expected-identities.json`, F9, skipped only under the live switch).

|---|---|---|

| footprint / pin-map digest | `footprintLibraryRevision` (asset version + content digest per tuple), `mappingRevision` (certificate ids) | same |
| routing / placement / DRC evidence digests | `rc01.digest = parity-<sha256(parity certificate)>`, `rc02.digest = route-<sha256(pass ids, DSN/SES digests, router versions, remaining/shorts/DRC/vias/length)>`, `rc03.digest = capability-<sha256(evaluation digest)>` (`qualification-evidence.ts`) | acceptance 7 |

Failure → nothing durable is written: no authority AND no routed board, at EVERY gate — the only durable write of a qualification is the single `commitQualification` commit (plus the lease aggregate), so a refusal at component authority, schematic authority, capability, routing, RC01, RC02 or the seal leaves the board head exactly as it was read (`b16-a-qualification-commit.test.ts`; parity suite; recovery H.6: a router crash leaves board and authority untouched, 500 `MFG_QUALIFY_FAILED` + a per-request correlation id, redacted trace). [B16-A corrected: F2 — "a failure at any gate leaves the board untouched" was false for gates after `commitSelected` until `3231f3f`]
