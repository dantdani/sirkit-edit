# Section B — resource authority integrity (envelope v2)

## Defect

`authorityStatus` was carried OUTSIDE the sealed digest: flipping a sealed
envelope from `verified` to `unverified` (or `unavailable`) after sealing was
undetectable (R3), and nothing required a consumed resource to be verified.

## Production changes

### `app/lib/engineering/resource-envelopes.ts`

- Schema bumped: `sirkit.read-authority-resource.v2` and
  `sirkit.read-resource-manifest.v2`.
- `contentDigest = engineeringDigest({ kind, authorityStatus, content })` —
  the status is INSIDE the pinned content identity.
- Manifest entries carry `authorityStatus` per resource, so a status flip
  breaks BOTH the envelope digest (`corrupt_resource`) and the manifest
  (`manifest_mismatch`).
- `validateResourceEnvelope` refuses v1 envelopes EXPLICITLY ("v1 is
  superseded by v2") — stale replay artifacts fail loudly, never silently
  pass under old semantics — and validates `authorityStatus` as one of
  `verified | unverified | unavailable`.

### `app/lib/engineering/tool-invocation.ts`

- New refusal statuses: `resource_unverified`, `resource_unavailable`
  (added to `READ_FAILURE_STATUSES`).
- After integrity validation, every CONSUMED authority kind is checked:
  `unavailable` → `resource_unavailable`; anything not `verified` →
  `resource_unverified`. Only verified resources are consumable — canonical
  AND auxiliary.

## Regeneration

All replay fixtures under `artifacts/ai-control-plane-b05*/`,
`ai-control-plane-b06*/`, `ai-control-plane-b07/` were regenerated byte-stably
for v2 digests (`scripts/generate-read-invocation-artifacts.ts`,
`scripts/generate-b06-schematic-artifacts.ts`); the byte-match suites pass
against the regenerated fixtures.

## Tests (`b09-2-closure.test.ts` §B)

1. Post-seal `authorityStatus` tamper → `corrupt_resource` +
   `manifest_mismatch` (R3).
2. Verified bundle read succeeds; a re-sealed (integrity-valid) unverified
   CANONICAL resource refuses `resource_unverified`; a re-sealed unavailable
   AUXILIARY resource refuses `resource_unavailable` — enforcement covers
   auxiliary resources, not just canonical.
3. A v1-schema envelope fails with the explicit superseded detail.
