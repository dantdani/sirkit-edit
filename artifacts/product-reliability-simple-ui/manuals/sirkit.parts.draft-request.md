## 5. Exact effects

- registry statement: Seal a REQUEST for an explicitly UNVERIFIED draft of ONE unresolved part from the documented facts it carries (exact MPN, manufacturer, package, pin table, pitch, body dimensions, pin-1 orientation, exposed pad), each given WITH its source label or listed as missing. Example input: {"refDes":"U1","exactMpn":{"value":"NE555DR","source":"TI SLFS022I, page 1"},"pinTable":[{"number":"1","function":"ground","source":"TI SLFS022I, table 5-1"}]}. A human files it as a publication request; it lists the missing facts, states that an unknown package variant, pad numbering, pin-1 orientation, pitch, courtyard or exposed-pad mapping BLOCKS PCB assignment, and claims no mapping certificate, approval or publication. It refuses without a pin table, exact MPN and manufacturer, with a fact lacking its source, or with conflicting facts.
- effect: the request is compiled against the editor's own refusal list and SEALED into a digest-pinned UNVERIFIED review task (`sirkit.part-draft-request.v1`, verification `unverified`) in the publication path's own vocabulary (AtomicPublicationSpec: exactMpn, profile, terminals, package, datasheet, manufacturer; symbol and footprint null); the documented facts with their sources, the missing facts and the C.4 PCB-assignment blockers (package variant (code and mounting); pad numbering (a pad for every pin); pin-1 orientation; pitch; body dimensions (courtyard); exposed-pad mapping) travel as data; side effects: `proposal-artifact` - nothing is filed, approved, published or verified
