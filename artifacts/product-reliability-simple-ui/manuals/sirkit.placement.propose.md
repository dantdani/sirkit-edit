## 3. Units and coordinate frame

- frame: +x right (declared by the descriptor text)
- frame: +y down (declared by the descriptor text)
- lengths and positions in millimetres (mm) (declared by the descriptor text)
- angles in whole degrees (declared by the descriptor text)
- rotation sense: counter-clockwise as seen on screen (clockwise subtracts) (declared by the descriptor text)
- fields suffixed `Mm` are millimetres: `maxDistanceMm`, `minDistanceMm`, `polygonMm`, `xMm`, `yMm`
- fields suffixed `Deg` are degrees: `rotationDeg`
- the placement intent (`intent`, sirkit.placement-intent.v1) is in millimetres (`units: "mm"`) in the board-world frame: the board's integer-nanometre world frame (app/lib/pcb/units.ts: +x right, +y DOWN, the KiCad file convention), written here in millimetres (up to 6 decimals; 1 nm resolution); the origin is the world origin (0, 0) - a rectangular outline's own `origin` (board.outline.origin, usually (0, 0)) is NOT subtracted; a component's position is its footprint origin (FootprintInstance.at); rotation is in whole degrees, positive COUNTER-CLOCKWISE as seen on screen (app/lib/pcb/geometry.ts: (1, 0) rotated +90 lands at (0, -1), screen up), restricted to 0 | 90 | 180 | 270; side bottom mirrors the footprint across x (x -> -x) before the rotation (transformPoint) (app/lib/pcb/placement/intent.ts)

## 5. Exact effects

An optional intent block (sirkit.placement-intent.v1: millimetres in the board frame, exact component and net identities) seeds the engine; where legalization, a lock, a keepout or a clearance changed what it asked, the candidate lists each conflict; a board over 50 physical instances is classified unsupported-scope in the candidate's data.
