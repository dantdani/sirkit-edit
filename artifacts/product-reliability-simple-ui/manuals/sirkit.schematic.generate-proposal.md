## 5. Exact effects

- registry statement: Generate a Build-mode schematic (real catalog parts) from a one-paragraph circuit request through the SAME planner, bounded build loops, merge and electrical check the Build composer's Generate runs, sealed into a proposal. Example input: {"prompt":"A 555 timer LED blinker powered from a 9 V battery through a 5 V linear regulator."}. The proposal carries the netlist, its completeness (complete, or partial with the omitted blocks), the electrical check's state (not checked is not a pass), the planner's state and the budget used, as data. Receiving it changes NOTHING: a human reviews it in the schematic review dialog and explicitly approves or discards it. One generation per run, within the run's remaining time; a failed generation is reported as data (failure class, provider reason, whether a retry can help), not as a proposal.
- effect: the planner, the bounded build loops, the merge and the advisory electrical check run once under the run's remaining deadline and the outcome is SEALED (`sirkit.schematic-generation-proposal.v1`): completeness, omitted blocks, the check's state, the planner's state and the budget spent travel as data; a failed generation is `proposal-blocked` data, never ok; side effects: `proposal-artifact` - nothing is applied
