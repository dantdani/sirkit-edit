## 5. Exact effects

- registry statement: Search Sirkit's local catalog by functional role, specifications and package text for exact published revisions that could resolve ONE instance (componentId), and sort every result into eligible (published, selectable, symbol + footprint + approved mapping, every connected pin mapped by function), draft, missing-asset, withdrawn, incompatible or unresolved, each with its reasons, warnings and the proved pin mapping. The instance's own part is considered first (binding its published revision changes no part). Example input: {"componentId":"D1","query":"small-signal fast switching diode 100 V 150 mA","functionalClass":"diode","limit":8}. At most 3 searches per turn. A hit is a candidate, never authority; no distributor is called; compatible is not electrical equivalence.
- effect: the local catalog is searched (the Build loop's own recall), every hit re-read through the catalog and the published evidence and sorted into eligible / draft / missing-asset / withdrawn / incompatible / unresolved with reasons and the proved mapping; side effects: `derived-cache` (the search's own memos); nothing is placed, bound, published or bought and no distributor is called
